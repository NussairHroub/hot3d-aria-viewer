How this page draws

The frame index is round(video.currentTime × fps) clamped to F−1, with the payload's own fps; row i of every array is frame i of the mp4. Boxes are released 2D boxes, stored with box_frame = "display", and a box may extend past the image edge or sit entirely outside it — the frame is fisheye and the released box is not clipped to it. Objects that leave the view, or that the annotation does not cover, appear as gaps rather than interpolated values.

Two signals are deliberately not drawn on the video. Eye gaze is given in the Central Pupil Frame as yaw, pitch and depth and is not projected into the image by this release, so it is reported as numbers only. Hand annotations carry a UmeTrack wrist pose plus 22 joint angles; the payload does not expand them into 3D fingertip landmarks, so only wrists, boxes and availability are shown. The hand wrists, boxes and masks on this page come from a separately licensed file, data/hands/<SEQ>.hands.json under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 where the rest of the recording is CC BY-SA 4.0, and MANO parameters are not republished in it.
